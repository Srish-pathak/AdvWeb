# Advocate Santosh Pathak – Legal Portfolio & Chamber Website

A responsive portfolio and client intake web application designed for **Advocate Santosh Pathak**. Built with legal industry aesthetics, accessibility, and Bar Council of India (BCI) regulatory compliance in mind.

---

## ⚖️ Features

- **Bar Council of India (BCI) Compliance Notice**: Integrated regulatory disclaimer and confirmation modal to adhere to rules regarding legal advertising and client solicitation.
- **Practice Area Showcase**: Dedicated sections detailing expertise across:
  - Civil Litigation & Property Disputes
  - Criminal Defense & Bail Matters
  - Constitutional & High Court Writ Petitions
  - Corporate, Contracts & Commercial Law
  - Family & Matrimonial Matters
  - Alternate Dispute Resolution (ADR) & Arbitration
- **Consultation Scheduling Form**: Chamber and virtual appointment intake form with urgency tags and dynamic confirmation modals.
- **Interactive Chamber FAQs**: Expandable accordion resolving common procedural and consultation queries.
- **Mobile Responsive Design**: Clean mobile navigation drawer, fluid typography, and touch-optimized layout.

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

- **HTML5**: Semantic document structure
- **Tailwind CSS**: Utility-first styling with custom legal color schemes (Deep Navy & Rich Gold)
- **Vanilla JavaScript**: Lightweight form handling, modal management, and mobile menu toggles
- **Lucide Icons / Heroicons**: Scalable vector UI iconography
- **Google Fonts**: `Playfair Display` (Headings) and `Plus Jakarta Sans` (Body text)
